Before you start.

Installing on your Mac

Unzip the download and move Chat Harvest to Applications. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later.

The app is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it and you choose to trust the app:

  1. Try opening Chat Harvest, then dismiss the warning.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and look for Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm that you want to open the app.

Prompts can vary, and managed Macs may restrict this option. See Apple’s instructions or the source-build guide.

Where is my Messages database?

Usually at ~/Library/Messages/chat.db. Follow the setup guide to make a consistent copy, then choose it with Open database….

macOS protects that folder; Terminal may need Full Disk Access to create the copy. Only messages already stored on your Mac are included. Chat Harvest reads the copy without changing it.

The copy is a snapshot, not a live sync. For newer messages, make a fresh copy and open it. Reload Database (⌘R) rereads the selected file; it does not fetch new messages from Messages or iCloud.

What stays private?

Everything stays on your Mac until you share an export. No account, analytics, or automatic AI uploads. Review message text before sharing; replacing speaker names doesn’t redact it.

What gets exported?

Messages in the database you open. Media uses placeholders such as [photo]. No iCloud-only history, deleted-message recovery, or media files.
